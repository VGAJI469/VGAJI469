## 🙋 About Me

Hi! I'm **Vansh Gajiwala**, a B.Tech CS (AI & ML) student at **SRM Institute of Science and Technology**, Kattankulathur 🎓 — with a passion for building systems at the intersection of **intelligence and engineering**.

My tech stack spans **React, Next.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Python**, and the full ML/AI toolchain. I architect end-to-end systems with equal attention to backend rigor and frontend polish.

My flagship project — the **AI Enterprise Council** — is a multi-agent decision intelligence platform where five C-suite AI agents (CEO, CFO, Legal, Marketing, PR) run on local LLMs via Ollama, debate using a structured engine, vote with credibility weighting, and evolve via a genetic replacement system. It features RAG via ChromaDB, CEO oversight governance, and a full FastAPI backend.

Beyond that, I've built a **QR-based grocery self-checkout system** (GrabNGo), a **campus food pre-ordering platform** (MealQ), and a **serverless Payment Reconciliation Engine** powered by GPT-4o-mini and AWS Lambda.

I'm also the **GSSoC 2026 open-source maintainer** for the AI Enterprise Council project, actively vetting contributors and growing the community.

I think in **systems** — every project I build has layers: architecture, implementation, documentation, and a story worth telling.
